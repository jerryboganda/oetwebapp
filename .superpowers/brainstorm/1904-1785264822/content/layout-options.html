A. Preset Bar (in-place)

One row of big preset buttons added to the top of the existing panel. Clicking a preset instantly ticks the right module checkboxes and scrolls to the relevant scope picker below. Nothing moves, nothing new to learn — just faster.

Effort: Small. Flow: Still one long page.

B. Guided Multi-Step Wizard

A focused modal/dedicated page with a progress bar: Step 1 pick a preset card, Step 2 shows only the customization that preset needs (e.g. which recall sets, expiry), Step 3 reviews a plain-English summary before Save. The full "everything at once" panel becomes an optional "Advanced" path.

Effort: Medium-large. Flow: Most guided, best for non-technical admins.

C. Hybrid: Quick Grant + Advanced Drawer

A prominent "⚡ Quick Grant" button opens a tiny focused popup: pick a preset card, confirm 1-2 smart defaults, done. The existing full panel stays put underneath, collapsed as "Advanced" for power-user tweaks (multi-plan, per-folder scope, etc).

Effort: Medium. Flow: Fast for the 90% case, full power still reachable.

My recommendation

Option C — it gives you the "one click and done" speed you asked for originally, keeps the flow dead simple for the common case, and doesn't hide the granular controls (folder/video/recall-set scoping) that already exist and that you'll likely want for other candidates later.
