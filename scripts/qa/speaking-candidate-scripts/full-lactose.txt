Based on what you have told me, I think you have a condition called secondary lactose intolerance.
When you had gastroenteritis, the infection irritated the lining of your gut. That lining makes an enzyme called lactase, which digests the sugar in milk.
While the lining is healing, your body cannot digest milk sugar properly, so dairy causes cramps, wind and diarrhoea.
I understand you feel it cannot be lactose intolerance because nobody in your family has it.
Actually, this type is not inherited. It is caused by the recent infection, so your family history does not affect it.
The good news is that it is usually temporary. It should settle within several weeks as the lining of your gut heals.
In the meantime, it would help to cut down on milk and dairy, or to try lactose free products.
