# Course Video Administration

## How the profession map works

- English videos are canonical and appear under all six professions.
- Arabic Listening and Reading videos are canonical and appear under all six professions.
- Arabic Medicine Writing and Speaking videos are canonical and appear under Medicine, Physiotherapy, Dentistry, and Radiography.
- Nursing and Pharmacy keep their own Arabic Writing and Speaking videos.
- Writing and Speaking always contain two operational folders: **Sessions** and **Workshops**.

Shared videos must not be uploaded repeatedly. Editing the canonical video from any profession updates every place where it is projected.

## Upload a video to a specific profession and folder

1. Open **Admin → Content → Video Library → Collections**.
2. Stay on the **Course Videos** view.
3. Select the required profession card.
4. Select **English** or **Arabic**.
5. Open **Writing** or **Speaking**, then find **Sessions** or **Workshops**.
6. Click **New** inside the required folder.
7. Click **Create draft & open wizard**.
8. In **Details**, enter the title and description. The profession, language, subtest, and course folder are already selected from the location where you clicked **New**.
9. Select a learner-facing category if required.
10. Optionally select a **Bunny collection** for physical storage.
11. Continue to the upload step, select the video file, wait for the upload to complete, and finish the remaining wizard steps.
12. Review and publish the video.

For shared content, select the source profession:

- English: any profession produces the same all-profession canonical scope.
- Arabic Listening/Reading: any profession produces the same all-profession canonical scope.
- Arabic Writing/Speaking for Medicine, Physiotherapy, Dentistry, or Radiography: selecting any of those four applies the shared four-profession scope.
- Arabic Writing/Speaking for Nursing or Pharmacy: select that specific profession.

## Move a video between Sessions and Workshops

1. Open **Course Videos** and locate the video.
2. Click **Edit / Move**.
3. In **Details**, change **Course folder** to **Sessions** or **Workshops**.
4. Save/continue the step.

This changes only its organisation metadata. It does not re-upload the file, replace the Bunny ID, duplicate the video, or erase learner progress.

## Move a video between Bunny storage folders

1. Open **Course Videos → Advanced / Storage**.
2. Select the current folder on the left.
3. Find the video on the right.
4. Use **Move**, choose the destination collection, and confirm.

For several videos, tick them and use the bulk move action. Videos can also be dragged onto a destination collection in the left folder tree.

## Create a new Bunny folder and upload into it

1. Open **Course Videos → Advanced / Storage**.
2. Click **New** to create a root folder, or select an existing folder and click **New sub-folder**.
3. Enter the folder name and save.
4. Return to **Course Videos**, click **New** in the correct profession/language/subtest/course folder, and create the draft.
5. In the **Details** step, select the newly created folder under **Bunny collection**.
6. Continue to upload and publish.

The profession/language/subtest/course-folder fields control where candidates see a video. The Bunny collection controls physical storage organisation. Changing one does not silently change the other.
