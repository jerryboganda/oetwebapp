Prepare for OET with Dr Ahmed Hesham through a structured digital learning platform designed for healthcare professionals.

The app gives enrolled learners convenient access to OET preparation content and practice resources on iPhone and iPad. Sign in with your existing account and everything you have enrolled in or purchased is right there - courses, materials, practice tools and video lessons, in sync with the web app.

FEATURES
- OET preparation courses and structured learning materials, including the Course Materials library.
- Listening Practice: computer-based Part A/B/C practice and full exams with full transcripts and review.
- Reading Practice: timed Part A/B/C and Full Exam practice with a simplified Practice Hub.
- Writing Practice: submit letters and case notes for AI-assisted feedback with model answers.
- Speaking Practice: record role-plays with microphone capture, playback and AI assessment.
- Practice Spelling and recall resources for exam-critical vocabulary.
- Profession-specific content for eligible healthcare professions.
- Shared AI credits with clear per-task costs shown before you use them.
- Secure video lessons streamed inside the app only, protecting course content.
- Account-based access across devices with device limits for your security.

WHO IT IS FOR
Doctors, nurses, pharmacists and other healthcare professionals preparing for the Occupational English Test (OET).

IMPORTANT NOTES
- The app is for enrolled learners: an account with eligible access or a purchased package is required. Course access and entitlements are managed through your account.
- Purchases and enrolments are handled securely on our website (oetwithdrhesham.co.uk) - there are no in-app purchases in this app. Existing subscribers simply sign in to access their content.
- Full account deletion is available from within the app.
- Independent preparation platform: OET with Dr Ahmed Hesham is an independent exam-preparation service. It is not the official OET test provider and is not presented as being operated by the official OET organisation.
