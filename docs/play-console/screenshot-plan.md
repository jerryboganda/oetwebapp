## Technical / QA rules
- Minimum Play: 2 screenshots. For this app: 6–8 strong phone screenshots.
- Recommended capture: `1080 x 1920` portrait (9:16), JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha.
- No device-frame mockups unless intentionally designed and policy-compliant.
- Do NOT reuse pre-fix screenshots. Large-phone top-bar/icon alignment + dashboard performance must be corrected FIRST.
- Verify the final set on a small Android phone AND a large phone (e.g. S24 Ultra).
- Tablets: test tablet rendering first; only then add tablet screenshots. Do NOT market tablet quality before verification.
- Keep source files organised as above — makes future listing updates much easier.
- Feature graphic (separate asset): `1024 x 500`, JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha, brand palette + `OET with Dr Ahmed Hesham`, minimal text, no #1/best/guaranteed-pass/discount/price/rank claims. See `public/brand/play-store/`.
